About

I am an undergraduate at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, studying networking and distributed computing. My interest is in the gap between what a protocol promises and what actually happens once packets leave the application: retransmissions, buffering, queueing and the latency that users feel.

Most of my work starts with a measurement. A streaming client is only useful if I can show its frame latency distribution; a word count is only interesting if I can explain why Spark finishes a job that Hadoop spends minutes on. This page collects the projects where I did both.

Projects

TCP vs UDP video streaming

A Python socket application that streams 720p video as JPEG frames, with length-prefixed framing over TCP and MTU-safe fragmentation over UDP. Frame latency, jitter and drop rate were measured for both transports and cross-checked against Wireshark captures.

  • Python
  • Sockets
  • Wireshark

MapReduce, Hadoop and Spark

A word-count pipeline implemented three ways: from scratch in standard Python, with Hadoop Streaming on a pseudo-distributed cluster, and with Spark. The same corpus was run at three sizes to compare execution models rather than just wall-clock time.

  • Hadoop
  • Spark
  • HDFS

Income classification with Spark MLlib

An end-to-end pipeline on the UCI Adult dataset: categorical encoding, feature assembly, cross-validated logistic regression and a tree ensemble, evaluated with precision, recall, F1 and AUC rather than accuracy alone.

  • MLlib
  • scikit-learn
  • Pandas
